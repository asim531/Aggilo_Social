questions

You're probably wondering.

Why is there a waitlist?
I'm building rooms based on who actually signs up. Every real person changes the map. I'd rather take my time and get the rooms right than rush and give you a noisy mess.
When will I get in?
When your room is ready. I'll reach out personally. No rush — I'd rather wait and give you something real than push you into a half-built space.
Is this just another AI chatbot app?
No. I'm not a chatbot you talk to for fun. I find clusters of people you should know and put you in a room together. After that, the humans do the talking.
How is this different from Discord / Slack / WhatsApp groups?
Those platforms give you tools to build communities. I build the community for you. No searching for groups, no dead channels, no managing notifications. You tell me what you need. I handle the rest.
What happens after I'm in a room?
Once you're in, I stay close. I watch how the conversation moves, how the group breathes. I keep the room relevant — surfacing things worth talking about, noticing when the energy shifts. The room is actively guided, not just maintained. You'll feel the difference.
Is it free?
Yes. Joining rooms, talking to people, and being matched by me costs nothing. This is what it is — no premium tier hiding behind what you actually need.
