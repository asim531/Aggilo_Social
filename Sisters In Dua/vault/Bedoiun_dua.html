The Bedouin & The Prophet (ﷺ)

Source: Sahih Muslim 2696 Grade: Sahih (Authentic) Narrator: Sa'd bin Abi Waqqas
The Context: A Bedouin (a desert Arab) came to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) and said: "Teach me a word that I can say (in remembrance of Allah)." The Prophet taught him the praises of Allah first. The Bedouin then paused and asked about his own needs.
Part 1: The Praise (For Allah)
لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ كَبِيرًا، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ كَثِيرًا، وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ الْعَزِيزِ الْحَكِيمِ
La ilaha illallah wahdahu la sharika lah,
Allahu Akbar kabira,
wal-hamdu lillahi kathira,
wa subhanallahi rabbil-alamin,
wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil-azizil-hakim.
There is no true god except Allah the One, who has no partner.
Allah is truly the Greatest,
and much praise is due to Allah,
and Glory be to Allah, Lord of the worlds,
and there is no power and no strength except in Allah, the All-Mighty, the All-Wise.
The Bedouin said: "These are for my Lord, what is for me?"
Part 2: The Request (For You)

The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Say the following..."

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي
Allāhumma-ghfir lī
1. O Allah, forgive me.
وَارْحَمْنِي
War-ḥamnī
2. And have mercy on me.
وَاهْدِنِي
Wah-dinī
3. And guide me.
وَارْزُقْنِي
War-zuqnī
4. And grant me provision (Rizq).
Note: In another narration (Sahih Muslim 2697), the Prophet (ﷺ) added: "For these words combine (the good of) this world and the Hereafter for you."
