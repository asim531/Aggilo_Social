The Comprehensive Dhikr

Al-Jawami' al-Du'a (Hadith of Abu Umamah)
Ref: Al-Mu’jam Al-Kabir (Tabarani), Sahih Ibn Hibban (830).
Grade: Sahih (Authentic) by Al-Albani (Sahih Al-Jami’ 2615).
Context: The Prophet (ﷺ) saw Abu Umamah moving his lips and asked, "What are you saying?" He replied, "I am remembering Allah." The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Shall I not indicate to you something better than your remembering Allah night and day?" He then taught him these words.
PART 1: AL-HAMD (Praise)
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا خَلَقَ
Al-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada mā khalaq
Praise be to Allah, the number of what He created.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ مِلْءَ مَا خَلَقَ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi milʾa mā khalaq
Praise be to Allah, filling what He created.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الأَرْضِ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-arḍ
Praise be to Allah, the number of what is in heavens & earth.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا أَحْصَى كِتَابُهُ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada mā aḥṣā kitābuh
Praise be to Allah, the number of what His Book recorded.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَلَى مَا أَحْصَى كِتَابُهُ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿalā mā aḥṣā kitābuh
Praise be to Allah, for what His Book recorded.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada kulli shayʾ
Praise be to Allah, the number of everything.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ مِلْءَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi milʾa kulli shayʾ
Praise be to Allah, filling everything.
PART 2: TASBIH (Glory)
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا خَلَقَ
Subḥān Allāhi ʿadada mā khalaq
Glory be to Allah, the number of what He created.
وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ مِلْءَ مَا خَلَقَ
Wa Subḥān Allāhi milʾa mā khalaq
