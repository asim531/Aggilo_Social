5 Prophets' Istighfar

Based on the lecture by Sheikh Samih Jad. These supplications are not just for sinners; they are powerful tools used by the Prophets to unlock blessings, relieve distress, and transform lives.

1. Prophet Adam (AS)
The Istighfar of Confession & Humility
رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
"Rabbana zalamna anfusana wa in lam taghfir lana wa tarhamna lanakunanna minal-khasireen."
"Our Lord, we have wronged ourselves, and if You do not forgive us and have mercy upon us, we will surely be among the losers."
Surah Al-A'raf (7:23)

Sheikh's Commentary:

This teaches us the power of confession. Unlike Shaytan, who was arrogant and refused to admit his fault, Adam (AS) immediately confessed his sin to Allah. The first step to changing your life is to stop making excuses, stop going to intermediaries, and confess your faults directly to the Almighty. We must realize that without His mercy, we are truly lost.

2. Prophet Nuh (AS)
The Istighfar of Surrender & Trust
رَبِّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَسْأَلَكَ مَا لَيْسَ لِي بِهِ عِلْمٌ ۖ وَإِلَّا تَغْفِرْ لِي وَتَرْحَمْنِي أَكُن مِّنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
"Rabbi innee aAAoothu bika an as-alaka ma laysa lee bihi AAilmun wa-illa taghfir lee wa tarhamnee akun minal-khasireen."
"My Lord, I seek refuge in You from asking that of which I have no knowledge. And unless You forgive me and have mercy upon me, I will be among the losers."
Surah Hud (11:47)

Sheikh's Commentary:

This Istighfar teaches us to trust Allah’s plan (Taqdeer). Sometimes we pray desperately for things (a specific job, a specific spouse) that might actually be harmful to us. We do not know the future, but Allah does. This prayer protects us from our own ignorance and helps us surrender, acknowledging that Allah knows what is best for us even when we do not.

3. Prophet Ibrahim (AS)
The Istighfar for Legacy & Salah
رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلَاةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِي ۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ
"Rabbij-alnee muqeema as-salati wa min thurriyyatee rabbana wa taqabbal duAAa. Rabbanagh-fir lee wa liwalidayya wa lil-mu'mineena yawma yaqoomul-hisab."
"My Lord, make me an establisher of prayer, and [many] from my descendants. Our Lord, and accept my supplication. Our Lord, forgive me and my parents and the believers the Day the account is established."
Surah Ibrahim (14:40-41)

Sheikh's Commentary:

This teaches inclusivity. Don't just pray for yourself; pray for your parents, your children (progeny), and the entire Ummah. Specifically, ask Allah for the ability to maintain Salah (prayer). The Sheikh notes that Istighfar acts as a "sealant" or a key to ensure our prayers are accepted. If you or your children struggle with prayer, use this Dua.

4. Prophet Yunus (AS)
The Istighfar for Distress & Anxiety
لَّا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ
"La ilaha illa anta subhanaka innee kuntu minaz-zalimeen."
"There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers."
Surah Al-Anbiya (21:87)

Sheikh's Commentary:

This is the ultimate cure for depression, stress, and anxiety. Prophet Muhammad (SAW) said any Dua made with this supplication will be answered. It combines three powerful elements: Tawheed (Oneness of God), Tasbih (Glorification), and Istighfar (Admitting faults). The Sheikh shared a story of a baker who recited this constantly, and as a result, every single one of his Duas was answered by Allah.

5. Prophet Sulaiman (AS)
The Istighfar for Abundance & Provision
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَهَبْ لِي مُلْكًا لَّا يَنبَغِي لِأَحَدٍ مِّن بَعْدِي ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْوَهَّابُ
"Rabbigh-fir lee wahab lee mulkan la yanbaghee li-ahadin min baAAdee innaka antal-Wahhab."
"My Lord, forgive me and grant me a kingdom such as will not belong to anyone after me. Indeed, You are the Bestower."
Surah Sad (38:35)

Sheikh's Commentary:

This teaches the formula for receiving blessings. Notice the order: Sulaiman (AS) asked for forgiveness first ("Rabbigh-fir lee"), and then asked for his worldly desire ("wahab lee mulkan"). If you want a job, a spouse, children, or wealth, do not just ask for the item. Make Istighfar first to clear your sins, and then ask Allah for what you need. This combination is the key to unlocking vast provisions.

May Allah accept our Istighfar and grant us the best in this life and the next.
