Supplication for Rectifying Affairs

Recited in times of distress and during the Morning/Evening Adhkar

يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ،
أَصْلِحْ لِي شَأْنِي كُلَّهُ،
وَلا تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ
"Yā Ḥayyu Yā Qayyūm, bi-raḥmatika astaghīth,
aṣliḥ lī sha’nī kullah,
wa lā takilnī ilā nafsī ṭarfata ‘ayn."
"O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting Sustainer, by Your mercy I seek assistance; rectify for me all of my affairs, and do not leave me to myself even for the blink of an eye."

Reference & Context

  • Primary Source: Authentic Recorded in Al-Mustadrak al-Hakim (1/545) and Sunan al-Nasa'i al-Kubra. Narrated by Anas bin Malik (may Allah be pleased with him).
  • Relation to Tirmidhi: The first part of this dua ("Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum, bi-rahmatika astagheeth") is found in Jami` at-Tirmidhi (Hadith 3524). The Prophet ﷺ would recite this specifically when sadness or distress befell him.
  • When to recite: It is recommended to recite this as part of the Morning and Evening supplications (Adhkar), or whenever one is feeling overwhelmed, anxious, or in need of Allah's help.
