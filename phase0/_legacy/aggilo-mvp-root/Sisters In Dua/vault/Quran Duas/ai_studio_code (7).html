The Quranic Supplications

Arabic, Transliteration, Pickthall Translation, Tafsir & Hadith

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201 General Well-being
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil 'akhirati hasanatan waqina 'adhaban-nar.
"Our Lord! Give unto us in the world that which is good and in the Hereafter that which is good, and guard us from the doom of Fire."
Context: A comprehensive prayer for success in both lives, often recited during Hajj (Tawaf) and daily prayers. Tafsir (Ibn Kathir): "Good in this world" includes health, provision, and beneficial knowledge. "Good in the Hereafter" includes Paradise and ease of reckoning. Hadith Reference: Anas (RA) reported that this was the supplication most frequently recited by the Prophet (ﷺ). (Sahih Al-Bukhari 6389)
Surah Sad 38:35 Leadership & Sovereignty
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَهَبْ لِي مُلْكًا لَّا يَنبَغِي لِأَحَدٍ مِّن بَعْدِي ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْوَهَّابُ
Rabbi-ghfir li wa hab li mulkan la yanbaghi li-ahadin min ba'di, innaka antal-Wahhab.
"My Lord! Forgive me and bestow on me sovereignty such as shall not belong to any after me. Lo! Thou art the Bestower."
Context: The prayer of Prophet Sulaiman (Solomon) asking for a unique miracle of kingship (control over wind and Jinn) to aid his mission. Tafsir: Sulaiman asked for forgiveness first, showing that spiritual cleansing precedes worldly requests. He asked for a kingdom unique to him as a sign of his Prophethood. Hadith Reference: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "A strong demon of the Jinn came to me... I wanted to tie him to one of the pillars of the mosque... but I remembered the invocation of my brother Sulaiman: 'My Lord! Forgive me and bestow on me sovereignty...'" (Sahih Al-Bukhari 3423)
Surah Al-Anbiya 21:87 Distress & Grief
لَّا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ
La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin.
"There is no God save Thee. Be Thou Glorified! Lo! I have been a wrong-doer."
Context: The Dua of Yunus (Jonah) while he was in the belly of the whale. Tafsir: It combines Tawheed (Oneness of God), Tasbeeh (Glorification), and Istighfar (Admission of guilt). Hadith Reference: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "No Muslim supplicates with it for anything, ever, but Allah responds to him." (Jami` at-Tirmidhi 3505)
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:286 Forgiveness & Ease
رَبَّنَا لاَ تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلاَ تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلاَ تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لاَ طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا
Rabbana la tu’akhidhna in-nasina aw akhta’na, Rabbana wa la tahmil 'alayna isran kama hamaltahu 'alal-ladhina min qablina, Rabbana wa la tuhammilna ma la taqata lana bih, wa'fu 'anna waghfir lana warhamna.
"Our Lord! Condemn us not if we forget, or miss the mark! Our Lord! Lay not on us such a burden as thou didst lay on those before us! Our Lord! Impose not on us that which we have not the strength to bear! Pardon us, absolve us and have mercy on us."
Context: The concluding verses of Surah Al-Baqarah. Hadith Reference: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surat Al-Baqarah at night, it will suffice him." (Sahih Al-Bukhari 5009). In Sahih Muslim, it is recorded that after each request in this Ayah, Allah replied: "I have done so."
Surah Ta-Ha 20:25-28 Confidence & Speech
رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي وَاحْلُلْ عُقْدَةً مِّن لِّسَانِي يَفْقَهُوا قَوْلِي
Rabbish-rah li sadri, wa yassir li amri, wahlul 'uqdatan min lisani, yafqahu qawli.
"My Lord! Open my breast for me, And ease my task for me; And loose a knot from my tongue, That they may understand my saying."
Context: Prophet Musa (Moses) praying before going to face Pharaoh. Tafsir: Musa asked for confidence (opening of the chest) and clarity of speech to convey the message effectively.
Surah Al-Furqan 25:74 Family & Offspring
رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا
Rabbana hab lana min azwajina wa dhurriyyatina qurrata a'yunin waj'alna lil-muttaqina imama.
"Our Lord! Vouchsafe us comfort of our wives and of our offspring, and make us patterns for (all) those who ward off (evil)."
Context: A description of the prayer made by the "Servants of the Most Merciful" (Ibad-ur-Rahman). Tafsir: "Comfort of our eyes" means seeing them obey Allah, which brings the greatest joy to a believer.
Surah Al-Qamar 54:10 Help in Desperation
أَنِّي مَغْلُوبٌ فَانتَصِرْ
Anni maghlubun fantasir.
"I am vanquished, so give help."
Context: The prayer of Prophet Nuh (Noah) after centuries of rejection by his people. Tafsir: A short, powerful admission of human weakness and total reliance on Allah's power. Following this Dua, the gates of heaven opened with pouring water (the Flood).
Surah Al-Imran 3:8 Steadfastness
رَبَّنَا لاَ تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً
Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmah.
"Our Lord! Cause not our hearts to stray after Thou hast guided us, and bestow upon us mercy from Thy Presence."
Context: A prayer for protection against doubt and deviation. Hadith Reference: Umm Salama (RA) asked the Prophet (ﷺ) why he supplicated so often: "O Turner of hearts, make my heart firm on Your religion." He replied that the heart is between two fingers of the Most Merciful. (Tirmidhi)
Surah Ibrahim 14:41 Parents
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ
Rabbana-ghfir li wa liwalidayya wa lil-mu'minina yawma yaqumul-hisab.
"Our Lord! Forgive me and my parents and believers on the day when the reckoning cometh."
Context: Prophet Ibrahim's prayer for his parents and the Ummah.
Surah Al-Qasas 28:24 Provision & Need
رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ
Rabbi inni lima anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqir.
"My Lord! I am needy of whatever good Thou sendest down for me."
Context: Prophet Musa (Moses) said this when he was alone, hungry, and homeless in Madyan. Tafsir: Immediately after this prayer, he was offered a job, a home, and a spouse. It is recited for employment and marriage.
Surah Az-Zukhruf 43:13-14 Travel
سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَٰذَا وَمَا كُنَّا لَهُ مُقْرِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا لَمُنقَلِبُونَ
Subhanalladhi sakh-khara lana hadha wa ma kunna lahu muqrinin, wa inna ila Rabbina lamunqalibun.
"Glorified be He Who hath subdued these unto us, and we were not capable (of subduing them); And lo! unto our Lord we are surely returning."
Context: The Quranic instruction on what to say when mounting a transport.
