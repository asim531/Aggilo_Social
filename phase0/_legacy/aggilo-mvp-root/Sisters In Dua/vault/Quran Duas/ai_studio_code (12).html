Al-Fatiha 1:6-7 Guidance
اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ * صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلاَ الضَّالِّينَ
Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim. Siratal-ladhina an'amta 'alayhim ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin.
"Show us the straight path, The path of those whom Thou hast favoured; Not the (path) of those who earn Thine anger nor of those who go astray."
Context: The opening prayer of the Quran.
Relevance: Recited at least 17 times a day in Salah. It is the most essential prayer for staying on the correct path in life and religion.
Hadith: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Al-Fatiha is the Mother of the Quran... and it is the Seven Oft-Repeated Verses." (Bukhari)
Al-Baqarah 2:127 Acceptance of Deeds
رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا إِنَّكَ أَنتَ السَّمِيعُ الْعَلِيمُ
Rabbana taqabbal minna innaka antas-sami'ul-'alim.
"Our Lord! Accept (this duty) from us. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Hearer, the Knower."
Context: Recited by Prophet Ibrahim and Ismail (AS) while they were raising the foundations of the Kaaba.
Relevance: Use this after performing any act of worship (Salah, charity, fasting) or a good deed to ask Allah to accept it despite its imperfections.
Al-Baqarah 2:128 Family & Faith
رَبَّنَا وَاجْعَلْنَا مُسْلِمَيْنِ لَكَ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِنَا أُمَّةً مُّسْلِمَةً لَّكَ وَأَرِنَا مَنَاسِكَنَا وَتُبْ عَلَيْنَا ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ
Rabbana waj'alna muslimayni laka wa min dhurriyyatina ummatan muslimatan laka wa arina manasikana wa tub 'alayna innaka antat-tawwabur-rahim.
"Our Lord! And make us submissive unto Thee and of our seed a nation submissive unto Thee, and show us our ways of worship, and relent toward us. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Relenting, the Merciful."
Context: Continuation of Ibrahim (AS)'s prayer for his lineage.
Relevance: For parents praying that their children and future generations remain steadfast on Islam.
Al-Baqarah 2:201 Comprehensive Good
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil 'akhirati hasanatan waqina 'adhaban-nar.
"Our Lord! Give unto us in the world that which is good and in the Hereafter that which is good, and guard us from the doom of Fire."
Context: Allah contrasts those who only ask for worldly gain with those who ask for the best of both worlds.
Relevance: The most all-encompassing Dua for success in life (health, wealth, family) and salvation in the afterlife.
Hadith: Anas (RA) reported that this was the supplication most frequently recited by the Prophet (ﷺ). (Bukhari & Muslim)
Al-Baqarah 2:250 Patience & Victory
رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا وَانصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
Rabbana afrigh 'alayna sabran wa thabbit aqdamana wansurna 'alal-qawmil-kafirin.
"Our Lord! Bestow on us endurance, make our foothold sure, and give us help against the disbelieving folk."
Context: Recited by the small army of Talut (Saul) when facing the massive army of Jalut (Goliath).
Relevance: Use when facing a massive challenge, an intimidating enemy, or a situation where the odds are stacked against you.
Al-Baqarah 2:286 Forgiveness & Ease
رَبَّنَا لاَ تُؤَاخِذْنَا إِن نَّسِينَا أَوْ أَخْطَأْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلاَ تَحْمِلْ عَلَيْنَا إِصْرًا كَمَا حَمَلْتَهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا وَلاَ تُحَمِّلْنَا مَا لاَ طَاقَةَ لَنَا بِهِ ۖ وَاعْفُ عَنَّا وَاغْفِرْ لَنَا وَارْحَمْنَا
Rabbana la tu’akhidhna in-nasina aw akhta’na, Rabbana wa la tahmil 'alayna isran kama hamaltahu 'alal-ladhina min qablina, Rabbana wa la tuhammilna ma la taqata lana bih, wa'fu 'anna waghfir lana warhamna.
"Our Lord! Condemn us not if we forget, or miss the mark! Our Lord! Lay not on us such a burden as thou didst lay on those before us! Our Lord! Impose not on us that which we have not the strength to bear! Pardon us, absolve us and have mercy on us."
Context: The concluding verse of Surah Al-Baqarah.
Relevance: Asking Allah not to punish us for honest mistakes or forgetfulness, and to not burden us beyond our capacity.
Hadith: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: "Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah Al-Baqarah at night, they will suffice him." (Bukhari)
Al-Imran 3:8 Firmness of Faith
رَبَّنَا لاَ تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً ۚ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ الْوَهَّابُ
Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmah, innaka antal-Wahhab.
"Our Lord! Cause not our hearts to stray after Thou hast guided us, and bestow upon us mercy from Thy Presence. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Bestower."
Context: The prayer of those "firm in knowledge" (Al-Rasikhuna fil-'Ilm).
Relevance: Essential for keeping one's faith intact in times of confusion (fitnah) and doubt.
Hadith: Umm Salama (RA) asked the Prophet (ﷺ) what his most frequent supplication was, and he replied: "O Turner of the hearts, make my heart firm on Your religion." (Tirmidhi) - This verse supports that sentiment.
Al-Imran 3:26 Sovereignty & Debt
قُلِ اللَّهُمَّ مَالِكَ الْمُلْكِ تُؤْتِي الْمُلْكَ مَن تَشَاءُ وَتَنزِعُ الْمُلْكَ مِمَّن تَشَاءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَاءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَاءُ ۖ بِيَدِكَ الْخَيْرُ ۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
Qulillahumma Malikal-mulki tu'til-mulka man tasha'u wa tanzi'ul-mulka mimman tasha'u wa tu'izzu man tasha'u wa tudhillu man tasha'u, biyadikal-khayr, innaka 'ala kulli shay'in qadir.
"Say: O Allah! Owner of Sovereignty! Thou givest sovereignty unto whom Thou wilt, and Thou withdrawest sovereignty from whom Thou wilt. Thou exaltest whom Thou wilt, and Thou abasest whom Thou wilt. In Thy hand is the good. Lo! Thou art Able to do all things."
Context: Revealed when the Prophet (ﷺ) promised the conquest of Persia/Rome, and hypocrites mocked the idea.
Relevance: Acknowledging Allah's control over all power. Specifically used for debt relief.
Hadith: The Prophet (ﷺ) taught Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) to recite this to clear debts, even if they were as large as a mountain of gold. (Tabarani)
Al-Imran 3:38 Pure Offspring
رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةً طَيِّبَةً ۖ إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ الدُّعَاءِ
Rabbi hab li min ladunka dhurriyyatan tayyibah, innaka sami'ud-du'a.
"My Lord! Bestow upon me from Thy presence a goodly offspring. Lo! Thou art the Hearer of Prayer."
Context: Zakariya (AS) prayed this after seeing the miraculous provision given to Maryam (AS).
Relevance: Primary Dua for those struggling with infertility or wishing for righteous children.
Al-Imran 3:53 To Be Recorded as Believers
رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا بِمَا أَنزَلْتَ وَاتَّبَعْنَا الرَّسُولَ فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشَّاهِدِينَ
Rabbana amanna bima anzalta wattaba'nar-rasula faktubna ma'ash-shahidin.
"Our Lord! We believe in that which Thou hast revealed and we follow him whom Thou hast sent. Enroll us among those who witness (to the truth)."
Context: The prayer of the Disciples (Hawariyyun) of Jesus (Isa AS).
Relevance: To renew commitment to the Prophet's path and ask to be counted among the truthful witnesses of Islam.
Al-Imran 3:147 Forgiveness in Struggle
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا وَإِسْرَافَنَا فِي أَمْرِنَا وَثَبِّتْ أَقْدَامَنَا وَانصُرْنَا عَلَى الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
Rabbana-ghfir lana dhunubana wa israfana fi amrina wa thabbit aqdamana wansurna 'alal-qawmil-kafirin.
"Our Lord! Forgive us our sins and our transgression of our duty, and make our foothold sure, and give us help against the disbelieving folk."
Context: The prayer of the righteous who fought alongside Prophets of the past; revealed in the context of the Battle of Uhud.
Relevance: When facing setbacks caused by one's own shortcomings, asking for forgiveness and strength to continue the struggle.
Al-Imran 3:191-192 Reflection & Protection
رَبَّنَا مَا خَلَقْتَ هَذا بَاطِلاً سُبْحَانَكَ فَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ * رَبَّنَا إِنَّكَ مَن تُدْخِلِ النَّارَ فَقَدْ أَخْزَيْتَهُ
Rabbana ma khalaqta hadha batilan subhanaka faqina 'adhaban-nar. Rabbana innaka man tudkhilin-nara faqad akhzaytah.
"Our Lord! Thou createdst not this in vain. Glory be to Thee! Preserve us from the doom of Fire. Our Lord! Whom Thou causest to enter the Fire, him indeed Thou hast confounded."
Context: The prayer of the "People of Understanding" (Ulul Albab) who reflect on the creation of the heavens and earth.
Relevance: Recited to affirm purpose in life and seek protection from Hellfire.
Hadith: It is reported that the Prophet (ﷺ) would recite the last ten verses of Al-Imran upon waking up for Tahajjud. (Bukhari)
An-Nisa 4:75 Dua of the Oppressed
رَبَّنَا أَخْرِجْنَا مِنْ هَٰذِهِ الْقَرْيَةِ الظَّالِمِ أَهْلُهَا وَاجْعَل لَّنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ وَلِيًّا وَاجْعَل لَّنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ نَصِيرًا
Rabbana akhrijna min hadhihil-qaryatiz-zalimi ahluha waj'al lana min ladunka waliyyan waj'al lana min ladunka nasira.
"Our Lord! Bring us forth from this town of which the people are oppressors! Oh, give us from Thy presence some protecting friend! Oh, give us from Thy presence some defender!"
Context: The cry of the weak men, women, and children trapped in Makkah during the period of persecution.
Relevance: For anyone living under oppression, tyranny, or in an abusive environment seeking a way out.
Al-Ma'idah 5:114 Sustenance
اللَّهُمَّ رَبَّنَا أَنزِلْ عَلَيْنَا مَائِدَةً مِّنَ السَّمَاءِ تَكُونُ لَنَا عِيدًا لِّأَوَّلِنَا وَآخِرِنَا وَآيَةً مِّنكَ ۖ وَارْزُقْنَا وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الرَّازِقِينَ
Allahumma rabbana anzil 'alayna ma'idatan minas-sama'i takunu lana 'idan li-awwalina wa akhirina wa ayatan minka warzuqna wa anta khayrur-raziqin.
"O Allah, Lord of us! Send down for us a table spread with food from heaven, that it may be a feast for us, for the first of us and for the last of us, and a sign from Thee. Give us sustenance, for Thou art the Best of Sustainers."
Context: The prayer of Isa (Jesus AS) when his disciples asked for a table spread from heaven.
Relevance: Asking for provision (Rizq) and blessings that bring joy to the community.
Al-A'raf 7:23 Repentance
رَبَّنَا ظَلَمْنَا أَنفُسَنَا وَإِن لَّمْ تَغْفِرْ لَنَا وَتَرْحَمْنَا لَنَكُونَنَّ مِنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
Rabbana zalamna anfusana wa in lam taghfir lana wa tarhamna lanakunanna minal-khasirin.
"Our Lord! We have wronged ourselves. If thou forgive us not and have not mercy on us, surely we are of the lost!"
Context: The words taught to Adam and Hawwa (Eve) by Allah to seek forgiveness after eating from the forbidden tree.
Relevance: The standard Dua for Tawbah (repentance) when one realizes they have committed a sin.
Al-A'raf 7:89 Seeking Judgment
رَبَّنَا افْتَحْ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَ قَوْمِنَا بِالْحَقِّ وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الْفَاتِحِينَ
Rabbana-ftah baynana wa bayna qawmina bil-haqqi wa anta khayrul-fatihin.
"Our Lord! Decide with truth between us and our folk, for Thou art the best of those who make decision."
Context: Prophet Shu'ayb (AS) asking Allah to judge between him and his disbelieving people.
Relevance: Used when seeking a resolution to a conflict, a court case, or asking Allah to "open" a way out of a deadlock.
Al-A'raf 7:126 Steadfastness in Death
رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا وَتَوَفَّنَا مُسْلِمِينَ
Rabbana afrigh 'alayna sabran wa tawaffana muslimin.
"Our Lord! Vouchsafe unto us patience and cause us to die as men who have surrendered (unto Thee)."
Context: The prayer of Pharaoh's magicians after they accepted the truth and were threatened with crucifixion.
Relevance: Asking for patience during severe persecution and a good end (Husn al-Khatimah).
Al-A'raf 7:151 Forgiveness for Siblings
رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِأَخِي وَأَدْخِلْنَا فِي رَحْمَتِكَ ۖ وَأَنتَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ
Rabbi-ghfir li wa li-akhi wa adkhilna fi rahmatika wa anta arhamur-rahimin.
"My Lord! Forgive me and my brother and cause us to enter into Thy mercy. Thou art the Most Merciful of those who show mercy."
Context: Musa (AS) prayed this after his anger subsided regarding the incident of the Golden Calf, seeking forgiveness for himself and his brother Harun.
Relevance: Praying for oneself and one's siblings, and seeking reconciliation after anger.
At-Tawbah 9:129 Ultimate Sufficiency
حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لاَ إِلَٰهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ ۖ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ ۖ وَهُوَ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ
Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa hu, 'alayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa Rabbul-'arshil-'azim.
"Allah sufficeth me. There is no God save Him. In Him have I put my trust, and He is Lord of the Tremendous Throne."
Context: The final verse of Surah At-Tawbah, instructing the Prophet (ﷺ) to rely on Allah if people turn away.
Relevance: The ultimate cure for anxiety and worry.
Hadith: The Prophet (ﷺ) said whoever recites this 7 times morning and evening, Allah will suffice him for whatever worries him in this world and the Hereafter. (Sunan Abi Dawud)
Yunus 10:85-86 Protection from Oppression
عَلَى اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْنَا رَبَّنَا لاَ تَجْعَلْنَا فِتْنَةً لِّلْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ * وَنَجِّنَا بِرَحْمَتِكَ مِنَ الْقَوْمِ الْكَافِرِينَ
Alallahi tawakkalna, Rabbana la taj'alna fitnatan lil-qawmiz-zalimin. Wa najjina birahmatika minal-qawmil-kafirin.
"In Allah we put our trust. Our Lord! Oh, make us not a lure for the wrongdoing folk; And, of Thy mercy, save us from the folk that disbelieve."
Context: The prayer of the followers of Musa (AS) when they were afraid of Pharaoh and his chiefs.
Relevance: Asking Allah not to make us a test for oppressors (i.e., don't let them defeat us, thinking they are right) and for safety.
Hud 11:47 Seeking Refuge from Ignorance
رَبِّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَسْأَلَكَ مَا لَيْسَ لِي بِهِ عِلْمٌ ۖ وَإِلاَّ تَغْفِرْ لِي وَتَرْحَمْنِي أَكُن مِّنَ الْخَاسِرِينَ
Rabbi inni a'udhu bika an as'alaka ma laysa li bihi 'ilm, wa illa taghfir li wa tarhamni akun minal-khasirin.
"My Lord! I seek refuge in Thee that I should ask Thee that whereof I have no knowledge. Unless Thou forgive me and have mercy on me, I shall be among the lost."
Context: Nuh (AS) prayed this after asking Allah to save his disbelieving son, and Allah corrected him.
Relevance: Seeking forgiveness for asking for things that may not be good for us, or for speaking without knowledge.
Yusuf 12:33 Protection from Sin
رَبِّ السِّجْنُ أَحَبُّ إِلَيَّ مِمَّا يَدْعُونَنِي إِلَيْهِ ۖ وَإِلاَّ تَصْرِفْ عَنِّي كَيْدَهُنَّ أَصْبُ إِلَيْهِنَّ وَأَكُن مِّنَ الْجَاهِلِينَ
Rabbis-sijnu ahabbu ilayya mimma yad'unani ilayh, wa illa tasrif 'anni kaydahunna asbu ilayhinna wa akun minal-jahilin.
"My Lord! Prison is more dear than that unto which they urge me, and if Thou fend not off their wiles from me I shall incline unto them and become of the foolish."
Context: Yusuf (AS) preferring prison over committing Zina (adultery) when seduced by the women of the city.
Relevance: Asking for protection when facing severe temptation or peer pressure to commit sin.
Yusuf 12:101 Good End
فَاطِرَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالأَرْضِ أَنتَ وَلِيِّي فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالآخِرَةِ ۖ تَوَفَّنِي مُسْلِمًا وَأَلْحِقْنِي بِالصَّالِحِينَ
Fatiras-samawati wal-ardi anta waliyyi fid-dunya wal-akhirah, tawaffani musliman wa alhiqni bis-salihin.
"Creator of the heavens and the earth! Thou art my Protecting Friend in the world and the Hereafter. Make me to die submissive (unto Thee), and join me to the righteous."
Context: Yusuf (AS) praying at the height of his power and reunion with his family.
Relevance: Praying for *Husn al-Khatimah* (a good ending) and to die in a state of Islam.
Ibrahim 14:35 Security of City & Faith
رَبِّ اجْعَلْ هَٰذَا الْبَلَدَ آمِنًا وَاجْنُبْنِي وَبَنِيَّ أَن نَّعْبُدَ الأَصْنَامَ
Rabbij-'al hadhal-balada aminan wajnubni wa baniyya an na'budal-asnam.
"My Lord! Make this city secure, and keep me and my sons away from worshipping idols."
Context: Ibrahim (AS) praying for Makkah and his children.
Relevance: Praying for the safety of one's country/city and protection from Shirk (polytheism) for oneself and children.
Ibrahim 14:40 Establishing Prayer
رَبِّ اجْعَلْنِي مُقِيمَ الصَّلاَةِ وَمِن ذُرِّيَّتِي ۚ رَبَّنَا وَتَقَبَّلْ دُعَاءِ
Rabbij-'alni muqimas-salati wa min dhurriyyati, Rabbana wa taqabbal du'a.
"My Lord! Make me to establish proper worship, and some of my posterity (also); our Lord! and accept my prayer."
Context: Ibrahim (AS) praying for himself and his lineage.
Relevance: Asking Allah to help you and your children maintain the daily Salah.
Ibrahim 14:41 Parents & Believers
رَبَّنَا اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِوَالِدَيَّ وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ يَوْمَ يَقُومُ الْحِسَابُ
Rabbana-ghfir li wa liwalidayya wa lil-mu'minina yawma yaqumul-hisab.
"Our Lord! Forgive me and my parents and believers on the day when the reckoning cometh."
Context: Ibrahim (AS) praying for the Ummah and his parents.
Relevance: A comprehensive prayer for forgiveness for oneself, parents, and the entire Muslim community.
Al-Isra 17:24 Mercy for Parents
رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا
Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira.
"My Lord! Have mercy on them both as they did care for me when I was little."
Context: A command from Allah on how to pray for parents.
Relevance: The essential Dua for parents, acknowledging their sacrifice in raising you.
Al-Isra 17:80 Honorable Entry & Exit
رَّبِّ أَدْخِلْنِي مُدْخَلَ صِدْقٍ وَأَخْرِجْنِي مُخْرَجَ صِدْقٍ وَاجْعَل لِّي مِن لَّدُنكَ سُلْطَانًا نَّصِيرًا
Rabbi adkhilni mudkhala sidqin wa akhrijni mukhraja sidqin waj'al li min ladunka sultanan nasira.
