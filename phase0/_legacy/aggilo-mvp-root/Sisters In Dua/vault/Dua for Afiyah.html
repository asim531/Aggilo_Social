Dua for Afiyah (Well-being)

The Prophet's Supplication for Complete Protection

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ العَفْوَ وَالعَافِيَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالآخِرَةِ،
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ العَفْوَ وَالعَافِيَةَ فِي دِينِي وَدُنْيَايَ وَأَهْلِي وَمَالِي،
اللَّهُمَّ اسْتُرْ عَوْرَاتِي، وَآمِنْ رَوْعَاتِي،
اللَّهُمَّ احْفَظْنِي مِن بَيْنِ يَدَيَّ، وَمِنْ خَلْفِي، وَعَنْ يَمِينِي، وَعَنْ شِمَالِي، وَمِنْ فَوْقِي،
وَأَعُوذُ بِعَظَمَتِكَ أَنْ أُغْتَالَ مِن تَحْتِي
"Allahumma inni as'aluka al-'afwa wal-'afiyata fid-dunya wal-akhirah,
Allahumma inni as'aluka al-'afwa wal-'afiyata fi dini wa dunyaya wa ahli wa mali,
Allahumma-stur 'awrati, wa aamin raw'ati,
Allahumma-hfazni min bayni yadayya, wa min khalfi, wa 'an yamini, wa 'an shimali, wa min fawqi,
wa a'udhu bi-'azamatika an ughtala min tahti."
"O Allah, I ask You for forgiveness and well-being in this world and in the Hereafter.
O Allah, I ask You for forgiveness and well-being in my religion and my worldly affairs, in my family and my wealth.
O Allah, conceal my faults and calm my fears.
O Allah, protect me from before me and behind me, from my right and my left, and from above me,
and I seek refuge in Your Greatness from being seized from beneath me."

📜 Reference

Sunan Abi Dawud (5074) and Sunan Ibn Majah (3871).

Authenticated (Sahih) by Sheikh Al-Albani.

Narrated by Abdullah ibn Umar (may Allah be pleased with them).

⏰ When to Read

This is a daily Adhkar (Remembrance) to be recited once in the morning and once in the evening.

  • Morning: Between Fajr and Sunrise.
  • Evening: Between Asr and Maghrib.

💡 Key Meanings

  • Al-Afiyah: Complete safety from illness, pain, and trials in faith.
  • Istur 'Awrati: Cover my faults/defects (so people don't see them).
  • Ughtala min tahti: Being seized from beneath (interpreted as earthquakes or the ground swallowing one up).

📖 Context

Ibn Umar (RA) stated that the Prophet ﷺ never abandoned these words. It is considered a "Fortress of Protection" covering all six directions (Front, Back, Right, Left, Up, Down) and all assets (Religion, Family, Money).
