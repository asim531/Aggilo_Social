Dua for Anxiety & Sorrow

The Supplication for the Removal of Distress (Al-Hamm wa Al-Hazan)

The Relevance

The Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) said: "No one is ever afflicted with distress or sorrow and says this supplication, except that Allah will remove his sorrow and replace his sadness with joy."

Arabic Text
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي عَبْدُكَ، ابْنُ عَبْدِكَ، ابْنُ أَمَتِكَ، نَاصِيَتِي بِيَدِكَ، مَاضٍ فِيَّ حُكْمُكَ، عَدْلٌ فِيَّ قَضَاؤُكَ، أَسْأَلُكَ بِكُلِّ اسْمٍ هُوَ لَكَ، سَمَّيْتَ بِهِ نَفْسَكَ، أَوْ أَنْزَلْتَهُ فِي كِتَابِكَ، أَوْ عَلَّمْتَهُ أَحَدًا مِنْ خَلْقِكَ، أَوِ اسْتَأْثَرْتَ بِهِ فِي عِلْمِ الْغَيْبِ عِنْدَكَ، أَنْ تَجْعَلَ الْقُرْآنَ رَبِيعَ قَلْبِي، وَنُورَ صَدْرِي، وَجَلَاءَ حُزْنِي، وَذَهَابَ هَمِّي
Transliteration

Allāhumma innī ʿabduka, ibnu ʿabdika, ibnu amatika,

nāṣiyatī biyadika, māḍin fiyya ḥukmuka, ʿadlun fiyya qaḍāʾuka.

As’aluka bikulli ismin huwa laka, sammayta bihi nafsaka,

aw anzaltahu fī kitābika, aw ʿallamtahu aḥadan min khalqika,

aw ista’tharta bihi fī ʿilmil-ghaybi ʿindaka,

an tajʿala al-Qur’āna rabīʿa qalbī, wa nūra ṣadrī,

wa jalā’a ḥuznī, wa dhahāba hammī.

English Translation

"O Allah, I am Your slave, the son of Your male slave, the son of Your female slave. My forelock is in Your Hand (i.e., I am under Your total control). Your command over me is forever executed and Your decree over me is just."

"I ask You by every Name belonging to You, which You named Yourself with, or revealed in Your Book, or You taught to any of Your creation, or You have preserved in the knowledge of the unseen with You..."

"...that You make the Quran the spring (life) of my heart, and the light of my chest, and the banisher of my sadness, and the reliever of my distress."

Reference: Musnad Imam Ahmad (Vol 1, 391, #3712).
Classified as Sahih (Authentic) by Sheikh Al-Albani.
