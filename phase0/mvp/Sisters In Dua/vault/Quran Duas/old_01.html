Selected Quranic Duas

Al-Imran 3:26 Sovereignty & Debt
قُلِ اللَّهُمَّ مَالِكَ الْمُلْكِ تُؤْتِي الْمُلْكَ مَن تَشَاءُ وَتَنزِعُ الْمُلْكَ مِمَّن تَشَاءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَاءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَاءُ ۖ بِيَدِكَ الْخَيْرُ ۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
Qulillahumma Malikal-mulki tu'til-mulka man tasha'u wa tanzi'ul-mulka mimman tasha'u wa tu'izzu man tasha'u wa tudhillu man tasha'u, biyadikal-khayr, innaka 'ala kulli shay'in qadir.
"Say: O Allah! Owner of Sovereignty! You give sovereignty to whom You will, and You withdraw sovereignty from whom You will. You honor whom You will, and You humble whom You will. In Your hand is [all] good. Indeed, You are over all things competent."
Context Revealed when the Prophet (ﷺ) promised the Muslims they would conquer Persia and Rome. The hypocrites mocked this, asking how a small desert group could defeat superpowers. This verse affirms that Allah alone controls power.
Relevance Debt Relief: The Prophet (ﷺ) taught Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA) to recite this to clear debts, even if they were as large as a mountain of gold. Also used to acknowledge Allah's control during political or workplace struggles. Reference: Al-Mu'jam al-Kabir (Tabarani); Authenticated in Sahih al-Targhib (1821)
Al-Imran 3:38 Pure Offspring
رَبِّ هَبْ لِي مِن لَّدُنكَ ذُرِّيَّةً طَيِّبَةً ۖ إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ الدُّعَاءِ
Rabbi hab li min ladunka dhurriyyatan tayyibah, innaka sami'ud-du'a.
"My Lord! Bestow upon me from Your presence a goodly offspring. Indeed, You are the Hearer of Prayer."
Context Prophet Zakariya (AS) was old and his wife barren. When he saw out-of-season fruits provided miraculously to Maryam (AS), he realized that if Allah can provide sustenance out of season, He can provide a child to an old man.
Relevance Infertility & Parenting: The primary Dua for couples struggling to conceive, and for parents asking Allah to make their children "Tayyibah" (pure and righteous).
Al-Imran 3:53 To Be Recorded as Believers
رَبَّنَا آمَنَّا بِمَا أَنزَلْتَ وَاتَّبَعْنَا الرَّسُولَ فَاكْتُبْنَا مَعَ الشَّاهِدِينَ
Rabbana amanna bima anzalta wattaba'nar-rasula faktubna ma'ash-shahidin.
"Our Lord! We believe in that which You have revealed and we follow the Messenger, so write us down among those who witness (to the truth)."
Context The prayer of the Hawariyyun (Disciples of Jesus/Isa AS). When Isa (AS) asked "Who are my helpers for Allah?", the disciples stood up, declared belief, and made this Dua.
Relevance Steadfastness: Recited to renew commitment to Islam, especially when feeling like a minority or surrounded by disbelief.
At-Tawbah 9:129 Ultimate Sufficiency
حَسْبِيَ اللَّهُ لاَ إِلَٰهَ إِلاَّ هُوَ ۖ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ ۖ وَهُوَ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيمِ
Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa hu, 'alayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa Rabbul-'arshil-'azim.
"Allah is sufficient for me. There is no God save Him. In Him have I put my trust, and He is Lord of the Tremendous Throne."
Context The final verse of Surah At-Tawbah. Allah instructs the Prophet (ﷺ) that if people turn away from the message, he should not despair but recite this phrase.
Relevance Anxiety & Worry: The Prophet (ﷺ) said whoever recites this 7 times morning and evening, Allah will suffice him for whatever worries him in this world and the Hereafter. Reference: Sunan Abi Dawud 5081
Al-Qasas 28:21 Safety from Oppressors
رَبِّ نَجِّنِي مِنَ الْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ
Rabbi najjini minal-qawmiz-zalimin.
"My Lord! Save me from the wrongdoing folk."
Context Recited by Prophet Musa (Moses AS) as he fled Egypt in fear for his life, having no direction, supplies, or protection after the accidental killing of an Egyptian.
Relevance Danger & Escape: Used when facing physical danger, lawsuits, tyrant bosses, or when trying to escape a toxic environment or relationship.
Ghafir 40:44 Entrusting Affairs to Allah
وَأُفَوِّضُ أَمْرِي إِلَى اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بَصِيرٌ بِالْعِبَادِ
Wa ufawwidu amri ilallah, innallaha basirun bil-'ibad.
"And I entrust my affair to Allah. Indeed, Allah is Seer of (His) servants."
Context Spoken by the "Believer from the family of Pharaoh" who hid his faith but eventually spoke up to defend Musa (AS). When threatened with plots, he resigned his outcome to Allah.
Relevance Overwhelming Stress: Used when the outcome is out of your hands. The Quran confirms immediately after: "So Allah protected him from the evils which they plotted."
