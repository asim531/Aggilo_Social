Du'a for the Soul That Feels Alone

When you have no one left — say this

يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ
أَصْلِحْ لِي شَأْنِي كُلَّهُ
وَلَا تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ

Transliteration

Yā Ḥayyu yā Qayyūm, bi-raḥmatika astaghīth,
aṣliḥ lī sha'nī kullahu,
wa lā takilnī ilā nafsī ṭarfata 'ayn

Translation

"O Ever-Living, O Sustainer of all existence —
by Your mercy I seek relief.
Rectify all my affairs for me,
and do not leave me to myself even for the blink of an eye."

Context & Relevance

This du'a was the supplication of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ himself — not reserved for ordinary moments, but brought out specifically in times of distress. When everything felt heavy and human support had its limits, this was his refuge.

The two Names invoked — Al-Ḥayy and Al-Qayyūm — are among the greatest Names of Allah. Al-Ḥayy means the one whose life is eternal and uninterrupted. Al-Qayyūm means the one who stands by Himself and upon whom all of existence depends every single moment. These names affirm a radical truth: while every human support can collapse, Allah's life and sustaining power never waver.

The closing phrase — "do not leave me to myself even for the blink of an eye" — is a confession of helplessness that becomes a source of strength. It acknowledges that the self, without divine guidance, is fragile. To say this is to hand the weight entirely over.

"Loneliness in Islam is not emptiness — it is a clearing. When every door closes, this du'a teaches you to knock on the only door that never closes."
