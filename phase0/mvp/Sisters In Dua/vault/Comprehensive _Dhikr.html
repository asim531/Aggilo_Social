Narrator: Abu Umamah Al-Bahili (May Allah be pleased with him).
Source Books: Al-Mu’jam Al-Kabir (Tabarani), Sahih Ibn Hibban (830), Musnad Ahmad (22144).
Authenticity: Graded Sahih (Authentic) by Sheikh Al-Albani in Sahih Al-Jami’ (No. 2615).

The Comprehensive Dhikr

The Story (Context)
The Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) passed by Abu Umamah (may Allah be pleased with him) while he was moving his lips.

The Prophet (ﷺ) asked: "What are you saying, O Abu Umamah?"
Abu Umamah replied: "I am remembering Allah."

The Prophet (ﷺ) then said: "Shall I not indicate to you something better [or more virtuous] than your remembering Allah night and day?"

He then taught him the following words:
Part 1: Al-Ḥamdu lillāh (Praise)
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا خَلَقَ
Al-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada mā khalaq
Praise be to Allah, the number of what He has created.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ مِلْءَ مَا خَلَقَ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi milʾa mā khalaq
And praise be to Allah, filling what He has created.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الأَرْضِ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-arḍ
And praise be to Allah, the number of what is in the heavens and the earth.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا أَحْصَى كِتَابُهُ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada mā aḥṣā kitābuh
And praise be to Allah, the number of what His Book has recorded.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَلَى مَا أَحْصَى كِتَابُهُ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿalā mā aḥṣā kitābuh
And praise be to Allah, for what His Book has recorded.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ عَدَدَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi ʿadada kulli shayʾ
And praise be to Allah, the number of everything.
وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ مِلْءَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ
Wal-ḥamdu lillāhi milʾa kulli shayʾ
And praise be to Allah, filling everything.
Part 2: Subḥān Allāh (Glory)
The Hadith concludes with: "Wa tusabbiḥullāha mithlahunna" (And you glorify Allah in the same manner). Below is the recitation for this part:
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا خَلَقَ
Subḥān Allāhi ʿadada mā khalaq
Glory be to Allah, the number of what He has created.
وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ مِلْءَ مَا خَلَقَ
Wa Subḥān Allāhi milʾa mā khalaq
And glory be to Allah, filling what He has created.
وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا فِي السَّمَاوَاتِ وَمَا فِي الأَرْضِ
Wa Subḥān Allāhi ʿadada mā fī al-samāwāti wa mā fī al-arḍ
And glory be to Allah, the number of what is in the heavens and the earth.
وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَدَدَ مَا أَحْصَى كِتَابُهُ
Wa Subḥān Allāhi ʿadada mā aḥṣā kitābuh
And glory be to Allah, the number of what His Book has recorded.
وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَلَى مَا أَحْصَى كِتَابُهُ
Wa Subḥān Allāhi ʿalā mā aḥṣā kitābuh
And glory be to Allah, for what His Book has recorded.
وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ عَدَدَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ
Wa Subḥān Allāhi ʿadada kulli shayʾ
And glory be to Allah, the number of everything.
وَسُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ مِلْءَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ
Wa Subḥān Allāhi milʾa kulli shayʾ
And glory be to Allah, filling everything.
