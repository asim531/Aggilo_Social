Sayyidul Istighfar

The Master of Seeking Forgiveness

Sahih al-Bukhari 6306
Arabic Text

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِي فَاغْفِرْ لِي، فَإِنَّهُ لاَ يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلاَّ أَنْتَ

Transliteration

Allahumma anta Rabbi la ilaha illa anta,
Khalaqtani wa ana 'abduka,
Wa ana 'ala 'ahdika wa wa'dika mastata'tu,
A'udhu bika min sharri ma sana'tu,
Abu-u laka bi-ni'matika 'alayya,
Wa abu-u laka bidhanbi faghfir li,
Fa innahu la yaghfiru adh-dhunuba illa anta.

English Translation

"O Allah, You are my Lord, none has the right to be worshipped except You. You created me and I am Your servant, and I abide to Your covenant and promise [to honor it] as best I can. I take refuge in You from the evil of which I committed. I acknowledge Your favor upon me and I acknowledge my sin, so forgive me, for verily none can forgive sins except You."

Establish Details & Virtues

Narrator: Shaddad bin Aus (may Allah be pleased with him)

Significance:

  • This is considered the "Master" (Sayyid) of repentance because it combines acknowledgment of Allah’s Divinity (Tawhid), confession of sins, and gratitude for blessings in the most perfect manner.

The Reward (Based on Hadith 6306):

The Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) said: "If somebody recites it during the day with firm faith in it, and dies on the same day before the evening, he will be from the people of Paradise; and if somebody recites it at night with firm faith in it, and dies before the morning, he will be from the people of Paradise."

Recommended Time: Once in the Morning and once in the Evening.

Reference: Sahih al-Bukhari, Vol. 8, Book 75, Hadith 318 (Intl. Numbering 6306)
