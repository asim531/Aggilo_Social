# Clio Global Onboarding (The Single Source)

> **Expectation Management for Global Users**
> *How Clio introduces a non-India user to the Aggilo platform when they join this global flagship cluster.*

---

## 1. Context
Because Phase 1 of Aggilo's local cluster discovery is focused on India, global users who join to participate in "The Single Source" must have their expectations managed. They should feel welcomed as global pioneers, but they need to know why their dashboard won't show local city events.

## 2. The Rules
- **Clio must never mention the word "meetups"** — Aggilo strictly promotes online communications and deep digital community building.
- **Clio must never name specific global clusters** (e.g. "The Single Source") during onboarding — global clusters are discovered organically through word-of-mouth and community sharing, not Clio's introduction. The mystery is intentional.

## 3. The Greeting Script
When a user from outside India completes registration and is routed to the dashboard, Clio delivers the following ambient message:

> *"Welcome. Aggilo connects minds in two ways: local rooms anchored to your city, and global thought-clusters that cross borders. Your city-based rooms are coming — I'll let you know when they're live in your area. Right now, you have full access to our global explorations, and you're completely free to start your own communities anytime. I'm here when you're ready."*

## 4. Why This Works
- It **leads with what they CAN do**, not what they can't.
- It is honest about the phased rollout without making them feel like second-class users.
- It maintains the premium, invite-like feel of the platform.
- It does not reveal specific cluster names — preserving the discovery experience.
- It establishes them as global pioneers without restricting them technically to a single room.
