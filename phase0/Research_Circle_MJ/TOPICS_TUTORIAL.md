# Topics Tutorial — Research Circle MJ

## What are Topics?
Topics are tags that organize posts by theme. Click a topic to filter the feed.

## How to See Topics
1. **Log in** to Research Circle MJ
2. Go to `/cluster` (the main feed)
3. Look **below the search bar** for the gray bar with "📁 All topics"

## How to Use Topics

### Filter by Topic
- Click any topic chip → feed shows only posts with that topic
- Click "📁 All topics" → see all topics in a tab
- Click "Clear filter" → back to all posts

### Create a Topic
**Method 1 — From the bar:**
- Click "+" button next to topic chips
- Type topic name → press Enter

**Method 2 — While posting:**
- Click "Topics" in PostComposer
- Click "+ Create topic" at bottom of dropdown
- Type name → Create

### Tag a Post
1. Write your post in PostComposer
2. Click "Topics" dropdown
3. Select existing topics or create new
4. Post → topics appear as chips on your post

### Click Topic on a Post
- Click any topic chip on any post
- Feed instantly filters to that topic

## Quick Tips
- Topics are shared across all members
- Create topics ad-hoc while posting
- Sage (AI) will suggest topics from papers
- Filter clears when you click "All topics" again
