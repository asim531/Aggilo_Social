🏠 Workflow 12: Premium Clusters — Make Your Crowd

Cold-start supply strategy for Atomic Crowd Leaders with existing micro-communities

PRD — Aggilo Social Network — Phase 1 Feature
Phase 1 Feature. Premium Clusters are available from Day 1 via the "Make Your Crowd" tab on the evangelist landing page. They serve as Aggilo's primary cold-start supply-side strategy — each approved Founder seeds 20–50 community members into a purpose-built room before public launch.
"Premium" = premium evaluation standards, NOT premium pricing. This feature is free in Phase 1. The premium is the credibility gate itself — it signals quality without requiring payment.

1. Problem Statement

Aggilo's current cold-start strategy relies on waitlist signups (demand-side) and Scout-seeded clusters (AI content, no real members). All six user archetypes are demand-side — people seeking connection. There is no supply-side archetype.

The consequence: New users arrive and see clusters with AI-generated discussion starters but zero real humans. They accurately identify this as a ghost town. Trust collapses before it forms.

The solution: Premium Clusters — a credibility-gated creation path for individuals with existing micro-communities. Each approved Founder brings their pre-formed social graph into Aggilo, producing real activity from Day 1.

2. The Seventh Archetype — The Atomic Crowd Leader

Identity
Someone who already influences 10–500 people around a topic — a natural convener, not a traditional "influencer"
Examples
College senior with study circle · Fitness enthusiast with 30-person WhatsApp gym group · Local food blogger · Chai meetup organizer
Psychology
"I already have these people. I wish there was a better way to organize around what we care about."
Cold Start Value
Each leader brings 20–50 pre-existing members. 10–20 approved leaders = 200–1,000 users in genuinely active clusters

5. Credibility Evaluation Framework

Core question: "Could this person's need be met by WhatsApp, Discord, or Telegram? If yes — why do they believe Aggilo serves them better?"

Scoring Dimensions

DimensionWeightWhat It Measures
Existing Community Evidence30%Does this person actually have people to bring?
Unmet Need Specificity25%Is the need genuinely unserved by existing platforms?
Demographic Coherence20%Do the AGGIL constraints match a real, identifiable group?
Platform Fit15%Does what they want align with Aggilo's soul — connection, not broadcasting?
Commitment Signal10%Will they actually follow through on populating the cluster?
The credibility score is NEVER shown to the applicant. The evaluation should feel like a conversation with Clio, not a test.

Approval Tiers

Custom Invite Link Onboarding

  1. Cluster preview — Name, purpose, member count, Founder recognition
  2. Simplified registration — Phone OTP → birth year + gender → language → nickname
  3. AGGIL gate check — Must pass hard constraints (location, language, age, gender)
  4. Auto-join — Immediately placed in the cluster after registration
  5. Clio greeting — "Welcome. [Founder nickname] built this room for people like you. Take a look around."
