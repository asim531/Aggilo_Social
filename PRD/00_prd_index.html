Executive Summary

Aggilo is an AI-native social network that creates purpose-based communities ("clusters") segmented by the AGGIL engine — Age, Gender, Geography, Interest, Language. Users discover clusters through AI suggestions, create clusters through conversation with an AI agent (Clio), and find relevant people through an AI matchmaker (Premium).

Positioning & Messaging Strictures:
  • Marketing must never lead with "AI" as the primary value proposition.
  • Terminology: The internal architectural term is "Cluster," but user-facing marketing and Clio's dialogue will lean towards using "Room" to describe these spaces, as it denotes a warmer, more human environment.
  • Valid Taglines: "Find the room you belong in. We handle the introductions." or "Stop shouting into the void. Let Clio find who is listening."

Core Product Decisions

Decision Chosen Rationale
Signup method Phone OTP India-centric, fast, reduces fake accounts
Identity Nickname only (no real names) Privacy-first, reduces harassment
Cluster limits Unlimited for all users No friction for engagement
Cluster deletion Not allowed Clusters persist and grow permanently
Member removal Not allowed No gatekeeping by creators
Purpose & Vibrant Tags Multiple per cluster Better discoverability
In-cluster content 4-Tab (Pulse, Timeline, Media, Members) Clean, persistent discussions + Clio-seeded content
DMs Available Enables deeper connections
Leave & Rejoin Both allowed, no cooldown Low friction
Premium free users Can see questionnaires + join private clusters Natural conversion funnel
Scout auto-create ≥90% relevance only Quality over quantity
Duplicate check Required before all cluster creation Prevents fragmentation
Moderation AI flag + human review; auto-ban for high risk Fast response + safety net
Notifications Web Push API Browser-native, no external dependency
Payments Razorpay (UPI) + Google Play Both channels for maximum reach
