The Problem Atlas Solves

⚠️ The Post-Join Gap: A user joins a cluster. They arrive to a feed with three posts — or zero. An implicit "now what?" hangs in the air. They don't know anyone. They haven't found a voice yet. The blank compose bar stares back. This is the highest-churn moment in any social platform.

Atlas closes this gap by making every cluster feel alive with relevant, timely, demographically-calibrated content — from the moment anyone joins. Atlas is invisible. Users never see "Atlas." They see Clio-surfaced content that feels chosen specifically for them. Because it was.

Three AI Agents — Roles & Relationships

🔍 Scout

Discovers clusters from trending topics. Auto-creates or seeds new communities.

Background worker 6h schedule Indirect

💬 Clio

Conversational orchestrator + cluster emotional host. Instructs Atlas and curates its output.

User-facing Orchestrator Editor-in-chief

🗺️ Atlas

Cluster content intelligence. Brings the outside world into each cluster, calibrated to its AGGIL demographic.

Background Clio-briefed Never user-facing

⚡ The "Now What?" — Post-Join UX

Design Principle: The post-join gap is a permission problem, not a content problem. New members don't post because they don't know if their voice is welcome. The conversation hook removes that friction — specific, relevant, tied to real content happening now.

Demographic Brief — How Clio Instructs Atlas

Clio builds and sends a structured JSON brief for every dispatch. Atlas only activates when briefed — it is never autonomous.

{
  "cluster_id": "uuid",
  "aggil_segment": {
    "age_range": [18, 24],
    "gender": "mixed",
    "geography": { "city": "Hyderabad", "area": "Gachibowli" },
    "interests": ["machine learning", "startup culture", "side projects"],
    "languages": ["English", "Telugu"]
  },
  "cluster_purpose": "Find co-founders for ML side projects in Hyderabad",
  "cluster_arc_phase": "A",
  "existing_pulse_topics": ["already shown topics — Atlas will exclude these"],
  "existing_post_titles": ["recent post titles for warm/reengagement context"],
  "freshness_threshold_hours": 48,
  "content_count_requested": 10,
  "variant": "cold"     // cold | warm | reengagement
}
Why demographic-first matters: The same headline about VC funding scores 96% for a 20-year-old student and 21% for a 38-year-old CTO looking at it from a board-level perspective. Atlas scores topic × demographic, not topic alone.

System Prompt Variants — Three Modes

🧊 Cold

post_count = 0 · Arc Phase A

Widely accessible topics. Low barrier — designed to make the first post feel safe and easy.

"If you were pitching to angels next month, what problem in your city would you actually solve?"
"What do you think about this funding news?"

🔥 Warm

post_count 1–5 · Arc Phase B/C

Builds on existing discussion. Atlas uses existing_post_titles to find thematically connected content.

"Everyone debates the right idea — this suggests the equity split conversation matters just as much. Where does your cluster land?"

⚡ Reengagement

72h silence · Arc regresses to C

One precision item. References a topic the cluster previously engaged with — not a fresh restart.

"The conversation you were having three days ago — this is what happened next."

Content Card — Scoring & Schema

How Atlas Scores

Each content item gets two independent scores via Llama 3 8B (Modal). Both must reach ≥80% or the card is discarded.

Relevance to interests94%
Demographic confidence91%
Below threshold — discarded71%
Quality gate: Atlas would rather return 0 cards than surface something irrelevant. Clio handles a zero-card result gracefully.

Guardrails

Atlas must never:

  • Surface cards below 80% relevance or 80% demographic confidence
  • Repeat a topic shown in the past 72 hours
  • Surface politically polarizing content
  • Generate hooks with manufactured urgency
  • Post directly to a cluster — Clio is the only voice
  • Return more than 10 cards per batch
  • Speak in first person — Atlas produces cards, not messages

Clio must never (with Atlas output):

  • Approve more than 3 cards per Pulse session
  • Use Atlas content in Posts feed if 2-message daily limit is reached
  • Present Atlas content as coming from "Atlas" — it appears as Clio-curated Pulse
  • Approve Phase A clusters cards marked safe_for_arc: ["warm"] only
  • Forward Atlas output to users without editorial review
Scout vs Atlas: Scout discovers clusters from the internet. Atlas discovers content for existing clusters. Scout = decides which restaurants to open. Atlas = the daily specials board inside each restaurant.
